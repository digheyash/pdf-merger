Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Our Privacy Commitment

Your privacy is our utmost priority. PDFMerge & Number operates as a 100% client-side application. All document processing—including reading, merging, reordering, and page numbering—occurs exclusively inside your local web browser memory. No document data is ever uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any external server.

2. Document Data Handling

When you select or drop PDF files into the tool:

  • Files are read into your device's temporary memory (RAM) via the HTML5 FileReader API.
  • No copies are created on any server, hard disk, or database.
  • Closing or refreshing your browser tab immediately and permanently purges all file data from your computer's memory.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use third-party advertising companies such as Google AdSense to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect non-personally identifiable information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Local Storage

We use browser localStorage strictly to store your interface theme preference (Dark or Light mode). No tracking cookies or personally identifiable information are stored in your local storage.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please reach out to us via our Contact Page.
