Your Privacy is Built-In, Not a Promise

Here is the technical architecture behind our zero-server privacy model:

1

Zero Network Transmission

Your PDF files are parsed directly into your browser's local memory via HTML5 FileReader. Not a single byte is uploaded over the internet.

2

In-Memory JavaScript Execution

Merging, reordering, and page numbering run 100% inside your browser using the local pdf-lib engine. Zero server computation.

3

Automatic Memory Eradication

We do not have a database. We store zero documents. The second you refresh or close this tab, your data vanishes completely.

4

Airplane Mode / Offline Test

Want proof? Turn off your Wi-Fi right now. This entire website will continue to merge and number your PDFs without hesitation!
